## PHOTOGRAPHER'S BRIEF — SPIRIT NOTE

*The visual language of Wine & Flow lives in the tension between restraint and abundance. Between stillness and pleasure. We are not photographing events — we are photographing a quality of attention. If a shot feels posed, reset. If a guest notices the camera, wait. The most important images in this brief are the ones where something true is actually happening. Trust the light, trust the space, and let the experience be the photographer.*
